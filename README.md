# Insurance-risk-Claim-analysis-power-bi-dashboard-
This project focuses on building an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze insurance policyholders and claim patterns. The goal is to transform scattered data into meaningful insights that help stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer demographics, vehicle details, and claim behavior, enabling better risk assessment and business strategy.

📂 Dataset

The dataset contains detailed information about:

Customer demographics (Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education, Income)
Vehicle details (Car Make, Model, Year, Usage Type)
Insurance data (Total Policies, Claim Amount, Claim Frequency)

It allows analysis of both customer segmentation and risk profiling.

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI Desktop – Data visualization & dashboard creation
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and measures
Gamma – Presentation (PPT) creation

Built relationships between datasets

DAX Calculations
Total Policies
Total Claim Amount

Average Claim Amount
Dashboard Development
Designed interactive visuals

Report & Presentation
Created a structured report

📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:
🔑 Key KPIs
Total Policies
Total Claim Amount
Claim Frequency
Average Claim Amount


📈 Visualizations
Car Use Analysis – Policy & claim distribution (Donut Chart)
Car Make Analysis – Risk by vehicle brand (Bar Chart)
Coverage Zone – Regional claim insights (Donut Chart)
Age Group Analysis – Claim trends by age (Histogram)
Car Year Trend – Impact of vehicle age (Area Chart)
Kids Driving Impact – Household risk comparison (Ribbon Chart)
Education Analysis – Customer segmentation (Pie Chart)
Education vs Marital Status – Risk heatmap (Matrix)


📈 Results & Insights
Identified high-risk segments based on age, vehicle type, and usage
Found that commercial vehicles and younger drivers have higher claim frequency
Discovered variations in claims across coverage zones
Highlighted how household factors (kids driving) influence risk
Enabled better customer segmentation and policy optimization
